Relaxation massage

Swedish massage, gentle fascial work and stretching to release tension in sore muscles, tendons and ligaments. Reduces pain, improves circulation and decreases stress and anxiety.

Therapeutic massage

Focused on a specific issue or injury, often chronic, to rehabilitate the body. Techniques and pressure vary with the therapist and the injury.
